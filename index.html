Country intelligence brief

News: official RSS feeds (only sources that have been verified and switched on), data from The GDELT Project (open data files and the DOC 2.0 API), each article linking to its original publisher. Indicators: World Bank Open Data. Conflict data: UCDP/PRIO. Boundaries: Natural Earth. Nothing on this page is generated by an AI model.

Publisher content. Headlines come from third-party publishers and are indexed by GDELT. This site shows only the headline, publisher name, date and a link to the original page. It does not reproduce article text or images. Publishers can ask for their content to be removed: open an issue in the project's GitHub repository. Some publishers are excluded, but a publisher that is not excluded has not necessarily approved or licensed this use. Attribution, the non-commercial nature of this site and this removal route do not by themselves give permission to reproduce headlines, and that right has not been conclusively established.

Known limitations
  • Topic labels come from fixed keyword matching on headlines. A keyword is not confirmed substantive coverage.
  • Media Coverage Patterns count the highest-ranked unique headlines, and the ranking rewards the same keywords. The proportions are not representative of all matching reports or of overall media coverage.
  • Country matching can produce false positives, for example a sea or an institution name.
  • The same story in different wording is counted as separate headlines. Syndicated copies are grouped only when the text matches. Repeats are not independent verification.
  • GDELT coverage is incomplete, and some of its files are missing in any given run. Most publishers have not been assessed for quality.
  • Conflict, governance and economic data are annual and lag by months or years. Governance uncertainty (WGI margins of error) is not retrieved.
  • There is no defensible diplomatic risk dataset in this project, so Diplomatic stays "Insufficient data".
  • The numbers were checked for internal consistency against the cached files, not independently against every original dataset or news item. This is an independent monitoring portfolio project, not a comprehensive geopolitical intelligence service, and it makes no forecasts.
